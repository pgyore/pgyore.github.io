Support

Answers to common questions, and how to reach us.

Is any real money involved?

No. Earnings, purses and P&L are paper money. The only purchases are the app's own upgrades, bought once through the App Store.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Profile and tap Restore purchase. Upgrades belong to your Apple Account, so they restore on any iPhone signed in to it.

Do I lose a round if I leave the app?

No. The app keeps your round in play, one for Practice and one for Career, and picks up where you left off.

What is a Placement ID?

The code for a private placement, such as $K7QX-9M2P-4A. It uses digits and letters except I, L, O and U, so it's easy to read aloud, and its last character is a check that catches a typo. The $ and dashes are optional when you type it.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. For private placement data, which is kept in iCloud, contact us below. See the privacy policy for details.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue at github.com/pgyore/pgyore.github.io/issues. Include your iPhone model and iOS version. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details.
