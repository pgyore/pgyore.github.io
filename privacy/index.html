Privacy Policy

Corporate Links has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.

On your iPhone

Your rounds, statements, Career progress, rating and preferences are stored only on your iPhone. They never leave it, except in your own device backups. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and handled by Apple. We never see your payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with the App Store which upgrades you own.

Private placements

Private placements are optional. If you create or subscribe to one, the app stores the following in the app's public iCloud database, which Apple operates (CloudKit):

  • the display name you choose;
  • an anonymous identifier that Apple generates for this app, which is not your Apple Account, name or email address;
  • the placements you created or subscribed to;
  • your orders and results in those placements, so that standings can be computed and checked.

Other subscribers to a placement see your display name, results and instrument set. Anyone with a Placement ID can look up that placement's details and standings. This data is used only to run placements. To have yours deleted, contact us through the support page.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no analytics, and its fonts are served from the site itself. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Corporate Links doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
